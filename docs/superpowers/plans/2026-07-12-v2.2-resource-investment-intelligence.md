# V2.2 Resource Investment Intelligence Implementation Plan

## Product Boundary

Resource Investment Intelligence is an independent enterprise plugin for contractors evaluating
resource-sector transformation and investment opportunities. Its system of record is Investment
Workspace under `<working-directory>/.agent-pi/business/investment/<project-id>/`.

It does not read or write Tender Workspace or Delivery Workspace private files. Cross-plugin use is
allowed only through immutable, hash-addressed, user-confirmed enterprise knowledge snapshots.

## Capability Packs

1. Mandate and Screening: investment mandate, opportunity thesis, stage gates, exclusion criteria,
   sponsor, jurisdiction, commodity, decision timetable and required diligence.
2. Resource and Technical Due Diligence: resource/reserve statements, confidence, mine or quarry
   plan, recovery, infrastructure, production, capex, technical gaps and independent verification.
3. Market and Offtake: demand, pricing, benchmark basis, logistics, customers, contracts, offtake,
   concentration, scenarios and evidence freshness.
4. Legal, Permitting and ESG: title, licences, permits, land, water, environment, social licence,
   closure, compliance, conditions precedent and accountable owners.
5. Financial Model and Valuation: approved assumptions, exact-decimal capex/opex/revenue, tax,
   working capital, financing, cash flow, NPV/IRR inputs, sensitivities and valuation scenarios.
6. Transaction, Financing and Investment Decision: structure, funding sources, covenants, security,
   conditions precedent, risks, mitigations, recommendation, approval and immutable audit history.

## Ordered Delivery

1. Add independent Investment Workspace schema, deterministic audit, direct-input fixtures and
   `investment_workspace` tool.
2. Add capability registry and `investment_capability` tool with optimistic revisions and staleness.
3. Implement capability packs in the order listed above, each with schema, audit, fixtures and Skill.
4. Add an independent Investment Workspace UI and investment-committee completion gate.
5. Add cross-plugin knowledge publication/import tests without direct private-store access.

## Acceptance Gates

- Investment Workspace initializes from direct user-selected investment sources with no Tender or
  Delivery Workspace present.
- Imported tender, delivery or knowledge data remains a frozen corroborating snapshot.
- A snapshot cannot be the sole basis for approved investment assumptions or decision approval.
- All approved assumptions and definitive findings cite active direct sources or locally verified
  and user-confirmed snapshots plus local review.
- Financial calculations use exact decimal arithmetic and preserve scenario identity.
- Investment mutations cannot resolve outside `.agent-pi/business/investment/<project-id>/`.
- Tests assert no tender or delivery store is created.
