# Agent Pi 2.2.0 / Agent π 2.2.0

Agent Pi 2.2.0 adds independent enterprise workbenches for tendering, project delivery, and resource investment. Each workbench keeps its own source boundary and audit model, while approved outputs can be published into the enterprise knowledge base for controlled reuse by the other workbenches.

Agent π 2.2.0 新增相互独立的投标、项目实施和资源投资企业工作台。每个工作台保持独立的来源边界与审计模型，同时可将经用户确认的成果发布到企业知识库，供其他工作台受控复用与相互印证。

## Highlights / 亮点

- **Tender intelligence** - Registers tender sources, mandatory requirements, deliverables, evaluation criteria, BOQ reconciliation, execution planning, schedule/resource planning, cost/cash-flow planning, and submission audit.
- **投标智能** - 管理招标来源、硬性指标、交付物、评分点、清单核对、实施策划、进度资源、成本现金流及递交审计。
- **Project delivery controls** - Builds implementation baselines and capability packs for contract scope, programme and progress, resources and procurement, cost and commercial control, cash flow, risk and change, and period reporting.
- **项目实施控制** - 建立实施基线，并覆盖合同范围、进度与实绩、资源采购、成本商务、现金流、风险变更及周期报告。
- **Resource investment intelligence** - Supports mandate screening, technical diligence, market and offtake, legal and ESG, financial valuation, and transaction decision workflows for contractor-to-resource-owner transformation.
- **资源投资智能** - 支持投资授权筛选、技术尽调、市场与包销、法律与 ESG、财务估值及交易决策，服务总包企业向资源型企业转型。
- **Controlled knowledge sharing** - Business artifacts are copied into immutable, hash-addressed enterprise knowledge storage only after explicit approval. Delivery and investment snapshots must match the approved registry and current file hash before import.
- **受控知识共享** - 业务成果仅在明确确认后复制到不可变、哈希寻址的企业知识库；实施和投资快照导入前必须匹配批准登记及当前文件哈希。
- **Independent source boundaries** - Delivery and investment workspaces can start from their own implementation or investment inputs and do not depend on tender-stage data.
- **独立来源边界** - 实施和投资工作台可直接使用各自输入启动，不依赖投标阶段数据。

## Safety Notes / 安全说明

Knowledge publications remain immutable. Cross-workbench evidence is corroborative and does not silently replace direct project, tender, or investment evidence. Fabricated, withdrawn, unconfirmed, missing, or modified snapshots are rejected before workspace state changes.

知识发布保持不可变。跨工作台证据用于相互印证，不会静默替代项目、投标或投资的直接证据；伪造、撤回、未确认、缺失或被修改的快照会在工作区状态变更前被拒绝。
